# BANK NEGARA MALAYSIA

**CIRCULAR**  
**To:** All licensed banks and prescribed financial institutions  
**Reference:** BNM/RH/CIR 028-12  
**Subject:** Operational Resilience & Fraud-Escalation Training Currency  
**Circular No.:** OR-TC 2026/1  
**Issued:** 1 September 2026  
**Effective:** 1 October 2026  

*Synthetic document for TUNTAS Grand Final demonstration. Styled after BNM circulars. Not an official Bank Negara issuance.*

---

## 1. Purpose

1.1 Instant payments and authorised-push-payment (APP) mule networks have shortened the window in which staff must detect, escalate and contain fraud-origin events.

1.2 Several in-force training programmes remain attendance-based and do not assess whether staff can execute the bank’s **2026 mule-account / APP-fraud escalation runbook**.

1.3 This Circular requires institutions to treat listed operational-training programmes as **not current** unless they are re-certified against the obligations below. Completion certificates issued against obsolete syllabi shall not be used as evidence of readiness after the effective date.

---

## 2. Application

2.1 This Circular applies to staff whose duties include any of:

- transaction monitoring or STR/escalation handling;
- automated fraud detection or fraud case handling;
- cyber / operational incident handling where the originating event is fraud or mule activity.

2.2 This Circular does **not** by itself re-open:

- personal-data protection (PDPA) operations training;
- AI ethics / AI-management-system competence training;
- enterprise risk and control-assurance training that does not certify fraud-escalation currency.

---

## 3. Obligations (training currency)

### OR-TC-3.1 Transaction-monitoring staff currency

Staff who operate transaction-monitoring or AML operational controls must, by 1 October 2026, complete role-based training that includes:

- mule-account typology current to 2026 instant-payment rails;
- documented escalation to fraud investigation within defined service levels;
- assessment **beyond attendance** (scenario or simulation).

Programmes that only cover generic CDD/STR without the 2026 mule/instant-payment module are **obsolete** for this obligation.

### OR-TC-3.2 Mule-account and APP-fraud case-handling currency

Staff who handle automated fraud alerts or fraud cases must, by 1 October 2026, complete training that includes:

- APP-fraud / mule-transfer playbooks;
- mandatory handoff to incident response within **60 minutes** of confirmed mule typology;
- maker-checker evidence for high-impact freezes.

Programmes that only cover generic automated detection without the 60-minute fraud-origin handoff are **obsolete** for this obligation.

### OR-TC-3.3 Incident-handler currency for fraud-origin events

Staff rostered as incident handlers for fraud-origin cyber or operational events must, by 1 October 2026, be assessed against the institution’s mule-escalation runbook.

Generic incident-handler certifications that do not assess that runbook are **obsolete** as sole evidence of readiness for this obligation.

---

## 4. Coordination

4.1 Compliance shall confirm interpretation of clauses 3.1–3.3 against the in-force training catalogue.

4.2 Learning & Development shall replace or remediate only programmes that fail currency. Programmes out of scope in 2.2 must be left unchanged.

4.3 Operations shall not roster all affected staff off the floor on the same day. Minimum operational coverage remains a hard constraint.

4.4 No procurement, scheduling or customer-facing roster change shall take effect before an authorised manager approves the remediation pack.

---

## 5. Evidence

5.1 After the effective date, attendance registers alone are insufficient. Institutions must retain traceable evidence: clause → control → competency → course → named role/staff → approval.

5.2 Where a programme is obsolete, prior approvals that relied on that programme as readiness evidence are stale and must be re-opened **only** for affected paths.

---

## 6. Effective date

This Circular takes effect on **1 October 2026**. Institutions shall complete impact assessment without delay.
