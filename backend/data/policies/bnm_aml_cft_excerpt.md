# BNM AML/CFT/CPF — Demo Clause Pack
# Version tag: 2024-02-demo

## AML-4.2 Customer Due Diligence
Staff must apply CDD/EDD proportionate to ML/TF risk and escalate unusual patterns through defined STR workflows.

## AML-5.1 Transaction Monitoring
Institutions shall implement monitoring systems and ensure operators can investigate alerts, document rationale, and avoid alert fatigue bypasses.

## AML-7.3 Training & Awareness
Relevant employees must receive role-based AML/CFT training with effectiveness assessment beyond attendance.
