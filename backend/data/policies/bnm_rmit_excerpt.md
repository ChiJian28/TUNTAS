# BNM Risk Management in Technology (RMiT) — Demo Clause Pack
# Source: BNM Policy Document (public). Version tag for TUNTAS: 2025-11-demo.

## RMiT-3.1 Technology Risk Governance
Financial institutions shall establish clear accountability for technology risk, including board and senior management oversight of technology-related controls supporting critical operations.

## RMiT-8.4 Cybersecurity Operations
Institutions must maintain detection, response and recovery capabilities proportionate to threat landscape, with trained personnel able to execute incident handling procedures.

## RMiT-10.2 Third-Party Technology Services
Outsourcing and third-party arrangements must include due diligence, data protection obligations, audit rights and exit strategies before production use.
