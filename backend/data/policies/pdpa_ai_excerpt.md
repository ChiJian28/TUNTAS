# PDPA + Malaysia AI Governance — Demo Clause Pack
# NSC07/TC17 maps to Standards Malaysia AI-related workstreams (ISO/IEC AI family alignment), not a single numbered Act.

## PDPA-S6 Security Principle
Personal data must be protected by practical security arrangements to prevent loss, misuse, modification or unauthorised access.

## PDPA-Breach-Notify
Material personal data breaches require timely assessment and notification pathways consistent with PDPA amendments and guidelines.

## AIGE-Human-Oversight
AI systems used in customer or risk decisions require human oversight, explainability proportionate to impact, and documented accountability.

## ISOIEC-42001-Competence
Organisations adopting AI management systems must ensure personnel competence for AI risks, including fraud analytics and automated decision support.
